Building tools to support embodied presence.

In our increasingly digital world, I care about technology that is in service of embodiment in our daily activities. I design and build software for the moments that happen off the screen — a teacher mid-lesson, a musician mid-session — built to seamlessly support a process, and to fall away as we weave our way back into our work in the world.

Becoming

For time, energy and money aligned with your values

What
A local-first companion for life development — money, the body, time and attention in one place, on a Mac and as a Home Screen app.

Report, never grade: counts and shares, never scores or streaks — enforced at the API, so no screen can render a grade.

Product design & build

Process

Listen, design, build.

Every project starts with listening: to the problem, the people, and what's needed beyond the brief. Then I design it and build it, until it feels calm and coherent to use — off the screen as much as on it.

  1. 01

    Listen

    • User research & surveys
    • Personas, user stories & flows
    • Competitive analysis
    • Usability testing

  2. 02

    Design

    • Interaction & visual design
    • Wireframes & prototypes · Figma
    • Type, colour & brand identity
    • Design tokens & systems

  3. 03

    Build

    • HTML · CSS · JavaScript
    • TypeScript · React · Electron
    • Web Audio API
    • AI-assisted development · Claude Code

About

Stu Pender

I'm a product designer in Chicago. I design interfaces and build them — HTML and CSS, React and TypeScript — with Claude Code as a daily pair.

The other half is music: a jazz degree, fifteen years of teaching, and a decade of production work for clients. Teaching is where I learned to make hard things simple. Production is where I learned to finish.

This is the product and design side. The studio and sound side lives at Being Sound.
