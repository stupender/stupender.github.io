Fretboard Constellations

For a teacher, mid-lesson

An interactive theory instrument for guitar. Scales, chords and harmony render as colour-coded, playable shapes across the neck, with notation and TAB.

Decision Design for an expert teaching live on a shared screen — a calm Lesson Mode, a full Studio Mode, and nothing locked behind skill level.

Archive

For a musician, mid-session

A local-first macOS app for re-discovering a large music library — random review, A–B looping at any speed, and up to five tracks layered at once.

Decision One test for every control: does it pull you out of the music? Modals became inline inputs; transport went keyboard-first.

Product design & build · TypeScript · Electron · Web Audio

For a newcomer, meeting a thirty-year practice

A landing page for Silvia Nakkach's 108-hour Certificate — live in about 48 hours, now the school's front door for the programme — and the trust that opened a fifteen-page follow-on engagement.

Practice

Listen, design, build — in one pair of hands.

Every project starts with listening: to the problem, the people, and what's needed beyond the brief. Then I design it and build it, until it feels calm and coherent in the hand — not just on the screen.

About

Stu Pender

I'm a product designer in Chicago. I design interfaces and build them — HTML and CSS, React and TypeScript — with Claude Code as a daily pair.

The other half is music: a jazz degree, fifteen years of teaching, and a decade of production work for clients. Teaching is where I learned to make hard things simple. Production is where I learned to finish.

This is the product and design side. The studio and sound side lives at Being Sound.
